Find a professor in the US who knows where you’re coming from

Faculty at US universities who were born, raised or educated in Pakistan. Start with your own university, then narrow by field.

Where do you study?

Professors who studied at your university are often the most willing to reply.

    Before you email a professor
    • Read one or two of their recent papers, and mention something specific from them in your email.
    • Ask one clear question they can answer in a few minutes. “Please guide me” is hard to reply to; “Is your lab taking PhD students in fall 2027?” is easy.
    • Keep it under 150 words. Say who you are, why them, and what you’re asking.
    • Write to each professor individually. Never send the same email to many professors at once.
    • If there’s no reply after two weeks, one short, polite follow-up is fine. After that, move on.
